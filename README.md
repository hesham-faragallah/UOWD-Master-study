# UOWD Master Study Hub

Study site for the University of Wollongong in Dubai Master of Digital Transformation:

- **MDT 901 – Digital Transformation Strategy & Industry 4.0** (Session 2)
- **MDT 904 – Research Methods & Design Thinking** (Chapters 1–7: research foundations, literature review, research design, qualitative and quantitative research, innovation fundamentals; design thinking sessions coming soon)

Each session has four tabs:

- **Topic** – the session explained in English and Arabic, section by section, with real-world examples
- **Infographic** – the one-page visual summary of the session
- **Quiz** – multiple-choice questions with instant feedback and a score by stage
- **Assignment** – Assessment 2 review article (English/Arabic) plus a Word download for MDT 901, and practice tasks for each MDT 904 chapter

Live site: https://hesham-faragallah.github.io/UOWD-Master-study/

The site is a single static `index.html` file with no build step.
